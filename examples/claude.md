# Claude (claude.ai and the Claude desktop app)

1. Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**.
2. Choose **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
3. Name it `Daysli` and paste `https://daysli.com/mcp`.
4. Choose **Connect**. Daysli's sign-in opens: sign in and approve the permissions you want.
5. In a chat, open **+**, then **Connectors**, and turn Daysli on.

Claude's free plan allows one custom connector. On Team and Enterprise plans an owner first adds the connector in
Organization settings.

Try: "What's on my Daysli calendar this week?" or "Set up my Daysli page."

Direct link: <https://claude.ai/customize/connectors>
