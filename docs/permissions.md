# Permissions

When an assistant connects, Daysli's consent screen lists each permission it asks for. You choose Allow or Deny.
An assistant never gets more than you allowed, or more than you could do yourself in Daysli.

| Permission | Lets the assistant |
| --- | --- |
| `profile:read` | Read your name, page address and what is set up |
| `setup:write` | Set up your page, card, bookable hours and meeting type, after you review it |
| `page.design:write` | Prepare, publish and restore your page's design, after you review it |
| `events:read` | Read your coming events and busy time (other calendars without titles) |
| `events:write` | Add and move your own events |
| `tasks:read` | Read your open to-dos |
| `tasks:write` | Add, reschedule and complete to-dos |
| `opportunities:write` | Turn your opportunity profile on, pause it or remove it |
| `introductions:write` | Find matching members, propose and answer introductions, and ask for a time |

There is no diary permission, and there never will be one.

## Your plan

An assistant acts with the same rules as the Daysli app. During your trial or a paid plan it can read and change
what you allowed. When the trial or plan has ended it can still read, but cannot change anything.

## Things written by other people

Text that other people wrote (an introduction, a page, a request) is passed to the assistant as information, never
as instructions.

## Disconnecting

Open [Your assistants](https://app.daysli.com/assistants?ref=gh-agent-connect) in Daysli and choose Disconnect. The assistant's tokens
are revoked and the connection stops on its next request.
